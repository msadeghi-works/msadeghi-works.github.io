Why Chenar

Built for adults who are busy, a little nervous, and serious about results.

  • Eight people, not twenty

    You speak in every lesson. Teachers know your name, your goal and your typical mistakes.

  • Teachers who know Persian speakers

    We target the classic traps: articles, the present perfect, “th”, and word stress. Native and Iranian teachers work as one team.

  • Campus or live online

    Same course, same materials. Switch between our Valiasr campus and online classes when life gets busy.

  • A clear plan from day one

    Start with a placement test, get a recommended course, and see your progress on the CEFR scale every term.
