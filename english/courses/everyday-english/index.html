About this course

We build on the basics with real tasks: booking a hotel, describing a problem, telling a story about your weekend. Weekly listening labs train your ear for natural speed.

By the end of the term you can…

  • Talk about past events and future plans
  • Handle travel situations on your own
  • Write short emails and messages
  • Understand simple podcasts
