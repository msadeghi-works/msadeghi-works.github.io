FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Talk to a human.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with 14 days free, no card required. You can also open the demo workspace right now from the login page.

Can my clients pay online?

Each invoice gets a secure payment link. Clients pay by card or bank transfer and the invoice is marked paid automatically.

What happens when I switch to yearly billing?

You pay for 12 months up front and save about 17%. Unused time is credited if you change plans.

Do you support VAT and multiple currencies?

Set a default tax rate per workspace and override it per invoice. Studio supports USD, EUR and GBP; Agency adds 30 more.

Can I import my existing clients?

Upload a CSV from your spreadsheet or old tool and Paperkite maps the columns for you.

Where is my data stored?

In EU data centres, encrypted at rest, with daily backups kept for 30 days.
